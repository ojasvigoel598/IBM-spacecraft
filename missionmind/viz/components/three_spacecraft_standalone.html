🔌 Pipeline: train.py → detect.py

train.py (offline, once):
- Loads run_normal.csv only (3600 rows)
- Adds d_temp/dt, d_volt/dt
- Adds 1W noise to constant solar (std=0 fix)
- Scaler: z=(x-mean)/scale (mean/scale printed)
- IsolationForest contamination 0.07, 300 trees
- Saves iforest.joblib + scaler.joblib + power/thermal ensemble

↓
detect.py (live, every second):
- Loads models
- Scales current window (120s)
- decision_function → anomaly_score (more negative = anomaly)
- predict → anomaly_flag 0/1
- OR ensemble power+thermal
- Output used by Granite + Three.js
